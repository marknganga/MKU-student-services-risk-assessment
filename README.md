# MKU Student Services: Initial Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

**Independent educational case study | Mark Nganga | October 2026**

A preliminary desktop assessment of potential cybersecurity risks affecting student services used by Mount Kenya University's Thika Main Campus community. It combines official public descriptions with historical user recollection and clearly labeled scenario assumptions.

> This is a portfolio simulation, not an MKU engagement, security audit, penetration test or vulnerability disclosure. No verified vulnerabilities or private student data are included. MKU has not commissioned or endorsed this work.

## Project contents

- [Full assessment](docs/initial-risk-assessment.md): scope, evidence, assets, scoring, six scenarios, treatment plan and limitations.
- [Risk register](risk-register.csv): editable record of assumptions, illustrative ratings, proposed controls and verification needs.
- [Evidence log](docs/evidence-log.md): sources, limitations and unresolved questions.

## Approach

1. Define a manageable student-services boundary.
2. Separate public facts, historical recollection and assumptions.
3. Identify information assets and confidentiality, integrity and availability needs.
4. Describe threat events, enabling conditions and business consequences.
5. Apply a transparent 3 × 3 illustrative scoring model.
6. Propose controls, owners and validation evidence.

The method is informed by NIST SP 800-30 concepts; it does not claim full framework implementation. Scores model assumed insufficient safeguards. They do not represent measured MKU risk. Actual current and residual risk remain unassessed.

## Key scope decisions

Hostel reservations are described at **building level**, based on historical recollection. Room allocation, editing and cancellation are not assumed. Disciplinary records are a **conditional extension**: their storage and portal integration are unknown. No network architecture or hosting location is inferred.

## Skills demonstrated

Asset identification; evidence handling; qualitative risk analysis; risk-register documentation; access control and recovery planning; communication of uncertainty and business impact.

## Limitations and next steps

No interviews, authenticated access, scans or configuration reviews were conducted. The next stage requires authorized owners to verify workflows, existing controls, and evidence. Re-score only after verification; publish no private records or operational security details.
